# MarginRx Product Plan

## Wedge

MarginRx starts as a 48-hour menu profit audit for independent restaurants, cloud kitchens, chef-led brands, cafes, and small QSR operators in India.

The promise: "We show exactly which dishes, channels, discounts, and platform fees are leaking profit, then give the owner a prioritized fix list."

## Ideal First Customers

- 1 to 5 outlet restaurants with delivery volume.
- Cloud kitchens dependent on Zomato and Swiggy.
- Chef-owner brands where recipes and margin are still managed in spreadsheets.
- Cafes and casual dining brands trying to scale but struggling with consistency and cost control.

## Pilot Offer

Charge Rs. 7,500 to Rs. 25,000 for the first audit depending on outlet complexity.

Inputs:

- Menu with current prices.
- Recipe cost sheets or ingredient quantity estimates.
- Vendor purchase bills for the last 30 days.
- Zomato and Swiggy payout statements.
- Monthly order counts by dish and channel.
- Packaging costs and average prep time per dish.

Output:

- Dish-level contribution margin.
- Aggregator leakage by item.
- Recommended prices for target margin.
- Discount and ad spend warning list.
- Direct-order opportunity estimate.
- Top 10 operational fixes ranked by rupees saved.

## SaaS Conversion

After the audit, convert owners to monthly software:

- Starter: Rs. 4,999 per outlet/month for menu margin tracking.
- Growth: Rs. 12,999 per outlet/month for delivery statement reconciliation and vendor price variance.
- Pro: Rs. 24,999 per outlet/month for multi-outlet controls, role-based SOPs, and owner alerts.

## Current Prototype Status

- Static app with menu margin audit, CSV import/export, owner report, print flow, and copyable summary.
- GST-aware assumptions for GST-inclusive menu pricing, output GST, GST on platform commission, and hidden input-tax burden.
- Pilot intake templates for menu data, vendor bills, and platform payout statements.
- Sales scripts and 48-hour pilot delivery playbook are ready for founder outreach.

## Immediate Field Test

Target 3 operators:

- One chef-owner casual dining brand.
- One cloud kitchen or delivery-heavy brand.
- One cafe/QSR with repeat customers.

Pilot success is not "software is complete." Pilot success is proving owners will pay for the leakage report and that the report identifies changes they are willing to execute.
