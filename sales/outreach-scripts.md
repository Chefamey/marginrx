# MarginRx Outreach Scripts

## WhatsApp DM

Hi Chef/Founder, I am building MarginRx, a chef-led profit audit for restaurants.

We check menu pricing, food cost, delivery platform fees, discounts, packaging, and prep labor to show exactly which dishes are leaking money.

I am taking 3 pilot restaurants this month. The audit takes 48 hours and gives you a clear owner report with top fixes ranked by rupees recovered.

Can I run this on your top 20 dishes?

## Short Call Pitch

Most restaurants know revenue, but not true dish-level contribution after food cost, packaging, staff time, discounting, ads, and platform commission.

MarginRx gives you an item-by-item leakage report. It tells you which dish to reprice, which discount to cut, and which delivery item should be pushed to direct ordering.

The first audit is fixed-fee. If the numbers are useful, we convert it into monthly tracking.

## Follow-Up

I can start with very rough numbers. Perfect data is not required for the first pass.

Send:
- Menu prices
- Food cost estimate per item
- Monthly orders
- Zomato/Swiggy statement
- Packaging cost

I will return a ranked leakage report in 48 hours.

## Objection: We Already Know Food Cost

Food cost is only one part. MarginRx looks at actual channel contribution after platform commission, discounts, ads, payment fees, packaging, prep labor, wastage, and hidden input-tax pressure.

Many dishes look profitable until channel deductions are added.

## Objection: Data Is Messy

Messy is normal. The first audit works with estimates and flags where better data is needed. The goal is not accounting perfection; the goal is finding the leaks big enough to act on.
