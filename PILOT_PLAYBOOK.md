# MarginRx Pilot Playbook

## Offer

MarginRx finds restaurant profit leakage in 48 hours.

The pilot is a chef-led audit of menu pricing, food cost, delivery platform fees, discounts, packaging, prep labor, and direct-order upside.

## Price

- Small cafe or cloud kitchen: Rs. 7,500
- Single full-service restaurant: Rs. 15,000
- Multi-brand or high-delivery outlet: Rs. 25,000

## Promise

"If we cannot identify at least 3 concrete profit leaks or pricing fixes, you do not continue into the monthly plan."

## Inputs To Collect

- Current menu with prices.
- Best available food cost or recipe estimate per item.
- Packaging cost per item.
- Monthly order count per item and channel.
- Zomato and Swiggy payout statement for the last month.
- Vendor bills for the last 30 days.
- Average prep time per dish.
- Current discount and ad spend assumptions.
- Whether menu prices are GST-inclusive.
- Applicable output GST rate and any platform commission GST assumptions.

## 48-Hour Delivery Flow

Day 0:
- Get payment.
- Collect files and owner context.
- Confirm top 20 to 50 dishes by revenue.

Day 1:
- Normalize menu and channel data.
- Run MarginRx audit.
- Flag bleeding, watch, and healthy SKUs.
- Identify direct-order candidates.

Day 2:
- Review the owner report.
- Prepare top 10 fixes ranked by monthly rupees.
- Separate net sales and estimated GST payable for the audited menu.
- Conduct 45-minute call with owner or founder.
- Offer monthly tracking plan.

## Founder Call Structure

1. Start with total visible leakage.
2. Show the top 3 dish-level leaks.
3. Show platform leakage and repeat-order opportunity.
4. Recommend price, discount, and portion changes.
5. Ask which fixes they can execute in the next 7 days.
6. Offer monthly tracking so leakage does not return.

## Close

"This first audit is a snapshot. The money is in making this a monthly control system: vendor price changes, platform payouts, and recipe costs checked before they silently damage margin."
